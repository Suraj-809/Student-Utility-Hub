Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Student Utility Hub handles information when you use our website and online tools.

1. Information We Collect

Most tools on Student Utility Hub are designed to work directly in your web browser. We aim to minimize the collection of unnecessary personal information.

Some features, such as the Contact Us form, may ask you to provide information such as your name and email address when you choose to submit a message.

2. Calculator and Tool Data

Information entered into calculators and other tools may be processed directly in your browser.

Unless a particular feature specifically states otherwise, the website does not need to store your calculator inputs on our servers.

3. Cookies

Student Utility Hub may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or support advertising services.

If third-party services are added, their own privacy policies may also apply.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as analytics, advertising networks, hosting services, or external libraries to operate and improve the website.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites.

6. Children's Privacy

Student Utility Hub is designed as a general-purpose educational resource. We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information associated with the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, its features, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Us page.
