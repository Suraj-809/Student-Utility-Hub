Compound Interest Calculator

Calculate compound interest and the final amount easily.

How is Compound Interest calculated?

Compound Interest is calculated using:

A = P(1 + R/n)ⁿᵗ

Compound Interest:

CI = A − P

Where:

P = Principal Amount

R = Annual Interest Rate

n = Number of times interest is compounded per year

t = Time in years

Example

For ₹10,000 at 8% annual interest for 5 years, compounded annually:

A = 10000 × (1 + 0.08)⁵
