Attendance Calculator

Calculate your attendance percentage easily.

How to Calculate Attendance?

Attendance percentage is calculated using:

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

Example

If you attended 45 classes out of 60:

(45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%
