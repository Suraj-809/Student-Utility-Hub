CGPA Calculator

Calculate your CGPA using subject credits and grade points.

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is calculated using the credits and grade points of each subject.

CGPA = Σ(Credit × Grade Point) ÷ Σ(Credits)

Example

Suppose you have two subjects:

Subject 1: 4 credits, Grade Point 8

Subject 2: 3 credits, Grade Point 9

CGPA = (4 × 8 + 3 × 9) ÷ (4 + 3)
