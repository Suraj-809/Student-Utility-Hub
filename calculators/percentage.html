Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages quickly and easily using this free percentage calculator.

How to Calculate Percentage?

To calculate a percentage of a number, use the formula:

Percentage Result = Number × Percentage ÷ 100

Example

If you want to calculate 20% of 500:

500 × 20 ÷ 100 = 100
