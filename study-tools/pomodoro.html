How to Use the Pomodoro Timer

Choose how long you want to study and how long you want your break to be. Start the timer and focus until the session ends.

How it works

  1. Set your study duration.
  2. Set your break duration.
  3. Click Apply Settings.
  4. Start your study session.
  5. A beep will play when the session finishes.
  6. The timer automatically switches to the next session.
