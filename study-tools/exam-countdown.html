How to Use the Exam Countdown

  1. Enter your exam name.
  2. Select your exam date.
  3. Select the exam time.
  4. Click "Start Countdown".
  5. Your remaining time will update every second.

Why use an exam countdown?

Knowing how much time remains can help you plan your revision schedule and stay focused on your preparation.
